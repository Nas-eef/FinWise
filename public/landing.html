Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install the APK on Android?
After clicking "Download APK", open the downloaded file on your Android device. If prompted, tap "Settings" and enable "Allow from this source" to complete the installation.
Does FinWise work offline?
Yes! FinWise is completely offline-first. You can record transactions, manage accounts, and adjust budgets without an internet connection. When you reconnect, data syncs seamlessly to your private Supabase cloud.
Is my financial data secure?
FinWise employs PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) and end-to-end user isolation. Only you have access to your budgets, accounts, notes, and documents.
Is FinWise completely free?
Yes, FinWise is 100% free with no hidden charges, paywalls, or intrusive banner advertisements.
